Features

Works on what you already own

No hardware

Odr is a web app. Install it from a link on any phone, tablet or laptop — Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Mac.

  • One login per person; stay signed in for the whole shift
  • Add to the home screen and it opens like an app
  • Light and dark mode; large touch targets for a busy floor
  • Shows when the internet drops so nobody keeps tapping a dead screen

The floor

Tables

Every table on one screen, colour-coded by what is happening at it.

  • Free, seated, sent to kitchen, ready to bill — at a glance
  • How long each table has been open and what it has ordered
  • Filters: only open tables, only tables waiting for the kitchen
  • Today's numbers at the top: settled, open, taken so far

Taking orders

Waiter's phone

Waiters take the order at the table and send it to the kitchen without walking.

  • Search finds any dish in two letters; photos and veg / non-veg marks on every card
  • A note per line for special requests — less spicy, no onion
  • Add more later: only the new items go to the kitchen
  • Open a table by mistake? Void it before anything is sent

Kitchen display

KDS

A screen or tablet in the kitchen replaces paper tickets.

  • Every KOT appears within seconds, oldest first
  • Colour bar turns red when a ticket is 12 minutes old
  • Tap Done when the food is out
  • Read-only kitchen login, so nobody changes an order from the pass

Bills and GST

Billing

Settle a table and the bill is ready — GST worked out per rate, invoice numbers in sequence.

  • CGST / SGST split per rate, never lumped together
  • Sequential invoice numbers per outlet, per financial year
  • Optional customer name and phone on the bill
  • Reprint any past bill; search invoices by number, name or phone
  • Scan-to-pay: add your UPI ID once in Settings and every printed bill carries a QR code with the exact amount filled in
  • Bills on the device (on request): hold Settle & bill for five seconds and that invoice is saved on the phone, not in the cloud. Settings shows the space used, with Sync to cloud and Clear
  • Odr does not take payments. Cash, UPI or card is settled at your counter as today

Parcels, Zomato and Swiggy

Off-table

Takeaway and aggregator orders flow through the same kitchen screen as dine-in.

  • One button for parcel, Zomato, Swiggy or other
  • Type the aggregator order number so the runner can match it
  • Same KOT, same kitchen display, same bill

QR ordering for diners

No app, no login

Guests scan the code on their table, browse the live menu and order. It lands straight in the kitchen.

  • Print the QR sheet once from Settings; one code per table
  • Photos, descriptions, veg / non-veg — the menu you maintain, live
  • A dish marked sold out disappears from the guest's menu instantly
  • Guests pay at the counter, as before

Your menu

One tap for sold out

The menu is a board, not a spreadsheet. Change it yourself, any time.

  • Sections and dishes with prices, photos, descriptions and GST rate
  • Sold out in one tap — the daily action, right on the card
  • Changes are live for waiters and diners the moment you save
  • Odr loads your first menu for you from a photo, PDF or spreadsheet

Printing

58 / 80 mm

Works with the thermal printer you already have.

  • Print bills and KOTs from any device through the browser
  • Or send KOTs straight to a network kitchen printer on your Wi-Fi
  • Pick 58 mm or 80 mm paper once; every slip fits

Sales and invoices

Reports

What came in today, what is still open, and yesterday to compare.

  • Settled today vs yesterday, still open on the floor, taken so far
  • Every invoice listed and searchable; refreshes every 15 seconds
  • All-outlets view for owners and managers of multi-outlet brands

Staff and roles

Owner, manager, cashier, captain, kitchen

Everyone signs in as themselves. Their role decides what they can see and do.

  • Add a person in a minute: name, email, password, role
  • Remove someone the day they leave; past orders are untouched
  • Full roles & access table

Several outlets, one account

Multi-outlet

Two or three branches of the same brand run on one Odr account.

  • Owners and managers see every outlet and switch from the outlet name in the top bar
  • Captains, cashiers and kitchen logins belong to one outlet, chosen when they are added
  • Each outlet keeps its own tables, QR codes, printer, GSTIN, address and invoice numbers
  • Share the brand menu across outlets — sold out is per outlet — or give an outlet its own menu
  • Sales across all outlets on one screen, with a total per outlet
  • New outlets are added by the Odr team; pricing is per outlet

Your brand, not ours

Branding

The app wears your restaurant's identity on every screen.

  • Logo, colours, font and light or dark theme, set by the owner
  • Applies to the waiter app and the diner's QR menu alike

Simple pricing, no surprises

Subscription

One yearly price per outlet. Everything above is included.

  • No setup fee, no per-device or per-user charges
  • The Odr team sets up your menu, tables and staff with you
  • A gentle reminder appears in the app a week before renewal
