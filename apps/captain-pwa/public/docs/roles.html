Roles & access

Everyone on your team signs in with their own email and password. Their role decides what they can see and do — nothing more, nothing less.

The five roles

Owner

Full control

The restaurant owner. Usually one person.

  • Everything a manager can do, plus:
  • Add and remove staff, choose their roles
  • Customise the app's branding — logo, colors, font, theme
  • See every outlet and switch between them from the top bar

Manager

Runs the floor

Your shift or floor manager.

  • Take, fire and void orders; settle and refund bills
  • Edit the menu — dishes, prices, photos, sold-out
  • Manage tables, table QR codes and printers
  • See sales, invoices and the staff list
  • See every outlet, switch between them, and view sales across all outlets
  • Cannot add or remove staff, or change branding

Cashier

Takes the money

Whoever sits at the till.

  • Take orders and fire KOTs to the kitchen
  • Settle bills and record payments
  • See sales and past invoices for their outlet
  • Cannot edit the menu, void orders or change settings
  • Works at one outlet — cannot see or switch to another

Captain

Serves tables

Waiters and stewards on the floor.

  • Open tables and take orders
  • Fire KOTs to the kitchen
  • Cannot settle bills or see sales
  • Works at one outlet — cannot see or switch to another

Kitchen

View only

The kitchen display (KDS) login — often a shared TV or tablet.

  • See incoming KOTs and mark them done
  • Cannot create orders or touch anything else

Diner

No login

Your guests. They never install anything or sign in.

  • Scan the table QR, browse the menu, place an order

More than one outlet

Outlets

Multi-outlet

One restaurant brand can run several outlets on one Odr account.

  • Owners and managers see every outlet and switch with one tap on the outlet name in the top bar
  • Captains, cashiers and kitchen logins belong to one outlet — set when the owner adds them
  • Each outlet has its own tables, QR codes, printer, GSTIN and invoice numbers
  • Outlets can share the brand menu (sold out is marked per outlet) or keep their own menu
  • Sales has an All outlets view for owners and managers
  • New outlets are added by the Odr team — pricing is per outlet
