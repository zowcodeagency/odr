What makes Odr different

Everything included, one price

No add-ons

Traditional POS systems sell the base billing software, then charge separately for extras.

  • Kitchen display (KDS), QR ordering, sales reports, staff logins — all included
  • No setup or onboarding fee; we set your menu and tables up for you
  • No per-device or per-user charges — add your whole team
  • Elsewhere: KDS, QR ordering and analytics are typically paid add-ons, with setup fees commonly in the thousands

No hardware to buy

Works on any phone

Odr is an app your team installs from a link — a thermal printer is all the hardware you need.

  • Runs on the phones and tablets you already own — Android, iPhone, iPad, any laptop
  • Waiters take orders at the table from their own phone
  • Your existing thermal printer (58/80mm) works for KOTs and bills
  • Elsewhere: billing counters usually mean a dedicated machine per outlet

Several outlets, one login

Multi-outlet

Running two or three branches? Owners and managers switch outlets from the top bar; floor staff belong to theirs.

  • Share one brand menu across outlets, with sold-out marked per outlet
  • Each outlet keeps its own tables, QR codes, printer, GSTIN and invoice numbers
  • Sales across all outlets on one screen

Diners order by QR — no app, no login

Included

Print the QR sheet once; guests scan, browse the live menu with photos, and order.

  • Menu changes go live for diners the second you save them
  • Orders land straight on the kitchen display
  • Mark a dish sold out in one tap — it disappears from the diner menu instantly

The app wears your brand

Unique to Odr

Not a generic billing screen — your restaurant's identity, everywhere.

  • Your logo, your colors, your font — the whole app restyles itself
  • Five looks (Classic, Gradient, Soft, Sharp, Glass) plus light and dark themes
  • Set once by the owner; every staff device follows automatically

Simple enough to learn in a shift

A real person sets you up
