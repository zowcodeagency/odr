Section 01

What Odr is

Four screens. One restaurant.

Odr replaces the billing machine, the KOT pad and the printed menu. Everything runs in a normal web browser — on a phone, a tablet, the counter computer or a TV in the kitchen. There is nothing to install.

ScreenWho uses itWhat it does
Captain Waiters, cashier, owner Open tables, take orders, fire the kitchen, settle and print the bill.
Kitchen Cooks A live list of what to cook. Tap Done when a dish leaves the pass.
QR menu Diners Scan the table code, browse the menu, order. No app, no login.
Admin Odr team only Sets up your restaurant and your subscription.
No payments inside Odr Odr never takes money. Diners pay at the counter — cash, UPI, card, however you already do it. Odr records what was ordered and prints the bill.

Words you will see

OrderEverything one table (or one parcel) has asked for.
KOTKitchen Order Ticket — the slip that tells the kitchen to start cooking.
FireSend the items to the kitchen.
SettleClose the order and produce the bill.
VoidCancel an order opened by mistake.
Off-tableParcel, Zomato, Swiggy — an order with no table.
Section 02

Starting your shift

Sign in once. Stay signed in all day.

  1. Open the Odr address in the browser. Bookmark it, or add it to the home screen so it opens like an app.
  2. Type your email and password. Tap Begin shift.
  3. If you work at more than one restaurant, pick the one you are in today.
  4. If your restaurant has more than one outlet and you are an owner or manager, pick the outlet you are in. Odr remembers it on this device.
  5. You land on the Tables screen. You are ready.

More than one outlet

One Odr account can run several outlets of the same brand. Everything that happens on the floor — tables, orders, kitchen tickets, bills and invoice numbers — belongs to one outlet.

Owner, managerSee every outlet. Tap the outlet name in the top bar to switch. The floor, kitchen and sales screens change to that outlet.
Captain, cashier, kitchenBelong to one outlet, chosen when the owner adds them. They sign straight into it and cannot see the others.
MenuOutlets usually share the brand menu. Sold out is marked per outlet, so a dish finished at one branch stays on sale at the next. An outlet can instead have its own menu — ask Odr when the outlet is set up.
SalesOwners and managers get an All outlets switch on the Sales screen with a total per outlet.
Adding an outletDone by the Odr team — pricing is per outlet. You then fill in its name, GSTIN, address and invoice prefix under Settings → Outlet.

The bar across the top

  • Outlet name and your email — check you are in the right place. With several outlets the name is a button: tap it to switch.
  • Connected / Working offline — tells you whether the internet is reachable.
  • Sun / moon — switches between light and dark. Dark is easier at night.
  • Icons — Tables, Kitchen, Settings, Sign out.
If a yellow strip appears Your subscription is ending within 7 days. Tell the owner to contact Odr. When it runs out the screen locks and no orders can be taken.

Who can do what

Each role is the one below it plus a little more. Nobody is ever blocked from serving a customer — the differences are only about money and settings.

RoleAdds on top of the row above
CaptainOpen tables, take orders, add items at any time, fire KOTs.
Cashier… and settle orders, make and print bills.
Manager… and void orders, edit the menu, change tables and printer settings. Can see the staff list.
Owner… and add or remove staff. The owner can do everything.
KitchenA read-only login for a kitchen screen. Cannot take or change orders.
A one- or two-person restaurant: sign in as the owner If the same person seats the table, takes the order, cooks or plates, and takes the money, they should use the owner login and nothing else. Owner includes every captain, cashier and manager ability, so one person runs the whole floor from one screen. Only create captain or cashier logins once you actually have separate staff and want to limit what they can reach.

There is no "switch role" button, and it would not help: an owner already has every ability a captain or cashier has. Switching down would only take abilities away. If a waiter is busy, the owner just does the task on their own screen.

Section 03

The floor

One screen that shows the whole restaurant. It refreshes itself every 5 seconds.

The four numbers at the top

OccupiedTables with an order running.
Ready to billTables whose food has been fired — likely eating now.
FreeTables you can seat someone at.
Open valueMoney currently sitting on the floor, unbilled.

Reading a table tile

  • FREE — nobody seated. Tap it to open an order.
  • Open — order started, nothing ordered yet.
  • Adding — items on the order, not yet sent to the kitchen.
  • Fired — the kitchen is cooking.
  • A green QR badge means the diner ordered from their own phone.
  • A number in the corner (46m) is how long the table has been running. Over 30 minutes the tile gets an outline — worth a look.

Filters

All · Occupied · Ready to bill · Free. During a rush, tap Ready to bill to see only the tables that may want the cheque.

Off-table strip Parcels and Zomato/Swiggy orders appear in their own row above the tables, so they never get lost between the tables.
Section 04

Taking an order

The screen every waiter lives in. It works with one thumb on a phone.

  1. Tap a free table. The order opens straight away.
  2. Pick a category (Dosa, Drinks, Snacks) or type in Search the menu — search looks at the whole menu, not just the open category.
  3. Tap + on a dish to add it. Tap again for a second one.
  4. Open the cart: on a phone, the bar at the bottom; on a computer, it is already on the right.
  5. Fix quantities with − / +, remove with the bin icon, and type any instruction in Note for the kitchen — "no onion", "less spicy".
  6. Tap Fire KOT. The kitchen sees it within 5 seconds.

The cart has two halves

Not sent yet

Still on your phone. You can change or delete anything here freely.

Sent to the order

Already with the kitchen and on the bill. It cannot be edited from here.

The customer wants one more thing

This is the most common thing that happens, and it is fully supported at any point until the bill is settled. A second round of drinks, an extra roti, a parcel on the way out — all the same steps:

  1. Tap the same table again (it will say Fired).
  2. Add the new dishes.
  3. Press Fire KOT again.

The kitchen gets a fresh ticket with only the new items — the first round is never sent twice. Everything lands on one bill at the end.

Two things staff notice While there are unsent items the tile goes back to Adding — the kitchen has not lost the first round, it just means something is waiting to be fired. And Settle & bill stays greyed out until you fire, so nobody bills food the kitchen never received. Waiters, cashiers, managers and owners can all do this.
Your phone locking will not lose the order Items you have added but not yet sent are saved on the device. Unlock, reopen Odr, and they are still there.

Opened a table by mistake?

A manager or owner taps Void order at the bottom of the cart and confirms. The table goes back to Free. A voided order is never billed.

Section 05

The kitchen screen

Put it on a tablet or a TV near the pass and leave it running.

Every fired KOT appears as a card, oldest first, and refreshes itself every 5 seconds. Nobody needs to touch a keyboard.

What is on a card

  • The table number, big enough to read across the kitchen.
  • The ticket number, e.g. KOT-0004, and a QR or ZOMATO badge if it did not come from a waiter.
  • Each dish with its quantity, and any note marked ✱.
  • The minutes since it was fired, and a coloured bar down the left edge.

The colour bar

GreenUnder 5 minutes — just came in.
AmberUnder 12 minutes — should be moving.
Red12 minutes or more — this table is waiting.

Clearing a ticket

When the food goes out, tap the big Done button. The card disappears. If you tap it by mistake the waiter can still see everything on the order — nothing is lost, the ticket just leaves the kitchen list.

Section 06

The bill

Settle, print, take the money at the counter.

  1. Open the table and press Settle & bill.
  2. The bill appears with GST worked out for you.
  3. Press Print to print on any printer connected to the device, or Kitchen printer to send it straight to the thermal printer over Wi-Fi.
  4. Collect payment at the counter. The table is free again.

What is on the bill

  • Restaurant name, address and GSTIN.
  • An invoice number that never repeats, e.g. CS/2026-27/00004.
  • Every dish with quantity and line total.
  • CGST and SGST shown separately for each tax rate. A 5% dish and a 12% dish get their own lines — that is what GST requires.
  • The grand total.
If the printer does not answer You will see "No printer answered". Check the printer is on and on the same Wi-Fi, then use the ordinary Print button instead. The bill is already saved either way — nothing is lost.

Paper size

Odr prints for the roll you actually own: a 58 mm roll or an 80 mm roll. Set it once in Settings and every bill and KOT comes out the right width.

Scan to pay

Put your UPI ID in Settings → Outlet and every printed bill ends with a QR code. The customer scans it with PhonePe, Google Pay or any UPI app and the exact bill amount is already filled in — no typing, no wrong amounts. Settings shows a preview so you can check it opens your account before you save.

Keeping a bill on the phone

Some restaurants ask us to switch this on. When it is on, hold Settle & bill for five seconds instead of tapping it. A bar fills across the button, the phone buzzes, and the bill is made on the phone itself — instantly, and it never goes to the cloud. The invoice number carries an L, e.g. CS/2026-27/LK7-00001, so it can never clash with the cloud's own numbers.

Settings → This device shows how many bills are on the phone, how many are not in the cloud, and the space they take. Sync to cloud sends them up with the same invoice numbers. Clear this device wipes them — it warns you first if any were never synced, because those are gone for good.

A bill on the phone is only on that phone Another device, and the Sales screen on any other device, will not show it until you sync. The kitchen-printer button is off for these bills; use Print.
Section 07

Parcels, Zomato and Swiggy

Orders with no table go through the same kitchen and the same bill.

  1. On the Tables screen tap Off-table.
  2. Choose Parcel, Zomato, Swiggy or Other.
  3. Type the customer's name (for a parcel) or the app's order reference (for Zomato and Swiggy).
  4. Tap Start order and add dishes exactly like a table.

It then appears in the Off-table strip on the floor screen with a coloured badge, and the kitchen sees the same badge on the ticket so they know it is going into a box, not onto a plate.

Zomato and Swiggy are typed in by hand Odr does not connect to their systems. Someone reads the order off the tablet and enters it here. The benefit is that everything — dine-in, parcel, aggregator — ends up in one kitchen queue and one set of numbers.
Section 08

QR ordering

The diner's own phone becomes the menu. Nothing to download.

What the diner does

  1. Points the phone camera at the QR card on the table.
  2. The menu opens, showing the restaurant's name and their table number.
  3. They browse by category, see the veg / non-veg square on every dish, and tap Add.
  4. They review the cart, can add a note, then confirm.
  5. They get a short order code and the message "Pay at the counter".

What you see

The order appears on the floor screen on that diner's table with a green QR badge, and the dishes go to the kitchen the moment the waiter fires the KOT. You settle and print it like any other table.

Prices cannot be tampered with The phone only sends which dish and how many. Every price and name comes from your menu on the server. A diner cannot change what anything costs.

Putting the codes on the tables

  1. Owner or manager opens Settings → Table QR codes.
  2. Taps Generate codes — one unique code per table.
  3. Taps Print sheet for an A4 page of cards, three to a row.
  4. Cut along the dotted lines and stand one on each table. Laminating them is worth it.
Print the cards once Every card is tied to its table. Print them after your tables are named the way you want them, so you do not have to reprint.
Section 09

Settings

Owners and managers only. Set it up once on day one.

Outlet

The outlet's name, GSTIN, address and invoice prefix — exactly what prints at the top of every bill. Each outlet has its own. Change them here and the next bill uses the new details. The UPI ID field puts a scan-to-pay code at the bottom of every bill (Section 06).

Tables

Type a range and Odr fills in the rest. T-1 to T-12 creates twelve tables. A comma list also works: Bar-1, Bar-2, Window. It shows you what it is about to add before you commit. The bin icon removes one.

Table QR codes

See Section 08. Generate, then print the sheet.

Printing

Paper width58 mm or 80 mm — match the roll you buy.
Kitchen printer IPThe printer's address on your Wi-Fi, e.g. 192.168.1.50. Leave blank if you only print through the browser.
PortAlmost always 9100. Leave it alone unless told otherwise.
Test printChecks the printer answers, before a real bill depends on it.

Staff

Shows everyone who can sign in. To add someone, fill in their name, email, a password of at least 8 characters, and pick a role (see Section 02). Give people the smallest role that lets them do their job. For a captain, cashier or kitchen login you also pick Works at — the outlet they belong to. Owners and managers see every outlet, so there is nothing to pick.

Removing someone. When a staff member leaves, tap the bin next to their name. They can no longer sign in. Their past orders and bills stay exactly as they were — nothing in your records changes.

Owner onlyAdding and removing staff. A manager can see the list but not change it.
YourselfCannot be removed — it would lock you out of your own restaurant.
The last ownerCannot be removed — somebody has to be able to manage the place.
When someone leaves, remove them the same day Their login works until you do. This is the one setting worth treating as security rather than housekeeping.

This device

Only shown when bills-on-the-phone is switched on for your restaurant. Space used, Sync to cloud, Clear this device — see Section 06.

Menu

The menu has its own screen — see Section 10. Odr loads your first menu when you join; after that you change it yourself.

Section 10

Editing your menu

Owners and managers. One board, one tap for the thing you do daily.

Open Menu from the side bar. Each section of your menu — Dosa, Drinks, Snacks — is its own row of dish cards, laid out like the board on the wall. Every change is live for waiters and diners the moment you save.

Sold out — the one you will use every day

  1. Open Menu.
  2. Find the dish and tap Sold out.

That is it. The dish disappears from the diner's QR menu and from the waiter's order pad straight away, and a diner who still has it in their cart is told it is unavailable rather than being billed for it. On the board the card stays put, greyed with a SOLD OUT tag, so the kitchen can see at a glance what is off. Tap Back on when it returns.

With several outlets sharing one menu, sold out applies to the outlet you are in only. The dish stays on sale at your other outlets.

Changing a price or a name

  1. Tap the dish card.
  2. Edit the name, price, GST rate, description or veg / non-veg.
  3. Save. New orders use the new price immediately.
Bills already printed do not change Every order stores the price it was sold at. Editing a dish never rewrites yesterday's takings.

Adding and removing

New dishTap Add dish at the end of a section.
New sectionType the name at the top right and tap Section.
Rename a sectionTap its title and type over it.
Delete a dishOpen the dish → Delete this dish. Past bills are untouched.
Delete a sectionOnly possible once it is empty — so a mis-tap cannot wipe half the menu.
Photograph a printed menu to load it in bulk For a big change, send Odr the menu and they import it in one go. Re-importing a menu with corrected prices updates the existing dishes — it will not leave you with each dish listed twice.
Section 11

When something goes wrong

Almost everything is fixed by reloading. Your orders live on the server, not on the phone.

What you seeWhat to do
"Working offline" The internet dropped. Check the Wi-Fi. Items you have added but not sent are safe; send them once it is back.
Sent back to Sign in The shift session expired. Sign in again — nothing is lost.
"Something broke on this screen" Tap Reload. If it comes straight back, tap Sign out and sign in again.
"Subscription ended" Contact Odr to renew. No orders can be taken until it is.
"No printer answered" Printer off, or on a different Wi-Fi. Use the plain Print button meanwhile.
A table is stuck occupied Manager opens it and taps Void order.
"No tables yet" Nobody has added the tables. Owner does it in Settings.
Diner says the QR does not work Serve them the normal way, then check the card is the one printed for that table.
The golden rule Anything that was fired or settled is stored on the server. Losing, dropping or reloading a phone never loses an order.
